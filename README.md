# AI Tool Disclosure in ISMIR

Since 2022, the usage of LLMs has increased steadily increased in how research is conducted,
the coding, plots, doing literature survey, formulating ideas, and to actually write the paper itself.
But how have various scientific societies reacted to this advancement, what policies have they
introduced, to what extent have they been followed. Organisations such as ACM ask authors
only to disclose AI usage apart from grammar and spell check, used in research while
organisations such as NIME (New Interfaces for Music Expression) require AI usage to be to be
declared even for grammatical usage.

This is what we are looking at!

- RQ1. How has the share of papers containing an AI-use disclosure changed between 2021 and
2026?
- RQ2. Where in the paper are disclosures placed, which tasks do they report (language editing,
translation, coding, literature search or review, data analysis, figures), and how specific are they
(tool named, extent of use, human verification)?
- RQ3. Do disclosure rates and content differ between a venue with no policy, a permissive policy and a mandatory requirement, and do disclosures match what each venue asks for?
